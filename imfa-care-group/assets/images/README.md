## Replacing with Genuine Business Images

When you are ready to upload your genuine company photographs:
1. Place your photo into this directory (`imfa-care-group/assets/images/`).
2. You can either overwrite the filename above (e.g. replace `hero-distribution.jpg`), OR
3. Keep your own filename (e.g. `imfa-store-front.jpg`) and update the `src` attribute in `index.html`.
